Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

NUANCE is responsible for your personal data. To ask anything about your data, email nuance.getcoloranalysis@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your first name, last name and email.
  • Order details: what you bought, the amount and the payment date. Our payment provider handles your card details, and we never see your full card number.
  • Your photos: the photos you send for your analysis, which show your face and hair.
  • Your questionnaire: your answers about your age range, features, hair, style and preferences.
  • Your messages: anything you send us by email.
  • Website data: pages visited, device, approximate location, and how you use the site, through the tools listed below.

Your photos

We use your photos only to create your report. We never sell or publish them, and we do not use them in our marketing without your separate written permission.

We delete your photos as soon as your report has been delivered. Please only send photos of yourself.

Who receives your data

We share data only with the service providers that help us run NUANCE, such as payment, questionnaire, email, hosting, website measurement, advertising and the software we use to prepare your report. We may also share data when the law requires it.

Some providers are based outside your country. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

How long we keep your data

  • Photos: deleted once your report has been delivered.
  • Questionnaire and report: only as long as we need them to answer your questions about your order.
  • Order and invoice records: as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Contact details of people who did not order: up to 3 years after your last contact with us.

Cookies and similar tools

Our website uses cookies and similar tools to measure visits and advertising, and for payment security. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, limit or object to how we use it, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email nuance.getcoloranalysis@gmail.com and we will answer within one month.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Age

NUANCE is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top of the page when we do.
