FAQ

Your questions

How does the online analysis work?

You answer a short quiz, upload 3 to 5 photos taken in natural light, and your color expert analyzes them. You receive your report and palette by email within 48 hours.

What if my hair is dyed?

No problem: we'll ask for a photo of your roots or of you before coloring, and take it into account.

Which photos should I send?

No makeup, facing the camera, next to a window during the day, without artificial light. You'll receive a detailed photo guide after ordering.

I already took a free online test. What's the difference?

A free test gives you a season name and a standard palette. NUANCE measures your own skin, eyes and hair, tests 200+ shades against your face, and gives you precise makeup, hair and style recommendations that fit your personality. In fact, 6 in 10 clients who had taken a free test discover their season was wrong.*

Does it work for every skin tone?

Yes. From very fair to very deep skin, with natural or dyed hair, every analysis is based on your own measurements, not on a generic chart.

Who sees my photos?

Only your color expert. Your photos are never shared and are deleted as soon as your report is completed.

Is it as accurate as an in-person session?

With well-taken photos, yes. If anything is unclear, we'll ask for extra photos at no cost.

Why online rather than an in-person session?

Same expertise, far less effort: no appointment to book, no travel, no two-hour session. You answer a short quiz, take a few photos at home in about 10 minutes, and get a detailed report you can reread anytime.
