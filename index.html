FAQ

Your questions

How does the online analysis work?

You answer a short quiz, upload 3 to 5 photos taken in natural light, and we analyze them. You receive your report and palette by email within 48 hours.

What if my hair is dyed?

No problem: we'll ask for a photo of your roots or of you before coloring, and take it into account.

Which photos should I send?

No makeup, facing the camera, next to a window during the day, without artificial light. You'll receive a detailed photo guide after ordering.

I already took a free online test. What's the difference?

A free test gives you a season name and a standard palette. NUANCE measures your own skin, eyes and hair, tests 200+ shades against your face, and gives you precise makeup, hair and style recommendations that fit your personality. In fact, 6 in 10 clients who had taken a free test discover their season was wrong.*

Does it work for every skin tone?

Yes. Whatever your skin color and undertone, from very fair to very deep, and including Black and mixed-heritage skin that generic charts often get wrong, your analysis is based on your own measurements, not on a generic chart. Natural or dyed hair, it works too.

Who sees my photos?

Only your color expert. Your photos are never shared and are deleted as soon as your report is completed.

Is it as accurate as an in-person session?

Our analysis combines digital color measurements with comparisons across your photos and color tests to assess your undertone, depth, intensity and contrast. We cross-check these observations rather than relying on a single image. Photo quality and lighting matter, so we'll ask for new photos if anything is unclear.

Why online rather than an in-person session?

Same expertise, far less effort: no appointment to book, no travel, no two-hour session. You answer a short quiz, take a few photos at home in about 10 minutes, and get a detailed report you can reread anytime. Your recommendations also take into account your personality and clothing style, from your quiz answers.
